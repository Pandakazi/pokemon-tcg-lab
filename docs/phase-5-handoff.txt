BASIC ENERGY

Special Energy and ambiguous Energy cards must remain normal functional cards and must not be collapsed into Basic Energy identity logic.

Do not silently rebuild or replace the existing certified Library/collection identity model. Add the deck-specific mapping deliberately and document any adapter required.


VALIDATION STATES

Use these user-facing states:

EMPTY
IN PROGRESS
VALID
INVALID

Rules:

EMPTY
- new/unsaved deck with zero cards.

IN PROGRESS
- unsaved deck containing fewer than 60 cards;
- no other validation failures are present.

VALID
- complete deck passing all applicable rules.

INVALID
- any actual deck-rule violation, regardless of save state.

Examples of immediate INVALID conditions include applicable:
- copy-limit violations;
- restricted-card violations;
- Standard legality violations;
- missing required Basic Pokémon;
- oversized deck;
- other authoritative rule-service failures.

SPECIAL SAVE RULE

Before a deck has been saved:

- fewer than 60 cards by itself does NOT make the deck INVALID;
- it remains IN PROGRESS if no other rules are violated.

Once a deck has been saved:

- fewer than 60 cards IS an INVALID condition.

Examples:

unsaved 43/60, otherwise legal
→ IN PROGRESS

unsaved 43/60 with copy-limit violation
→ INVALID

saved 43/60, otherwise legal
→ INVALID
→ useful reason such as "17 cards remaining"

60/60 and legal
→ VALID

A real rule violation always overrides IN PROGRESS.

Validation messages must be concise and actionable.

Do not show only "INVALID" when useful reasons can be surfaced.
